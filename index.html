Type !play. That's the setup.

Chat Play connects your chat bot to any HOS game with one command. Viewers join, guess, bid and vote right from chat. Switch games anytime without touching your bot again.

Already running live on independent streams.

Set up Chat Play
streamer switched the game to Bingo
nightowl_k: !play
HOSbot: nightowl_k is in. Card 14 dealt.
rustbucket: !play
HOSbot: rustbucket is in. Card 15 dealt.
kitkat_qa: !play B7
HOSbot: B7 marked. One away, kitkat_qa.
